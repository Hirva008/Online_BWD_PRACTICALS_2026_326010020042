About Marwadi University

Center of Academic Excellence in Saurashtra

Established under the Gujarat Private Universities Act, Marwadi University has rapidly emerged as a premier institution in western India. Situated on Rajkot-Morbi Highway, MU delivers industry-aligned engineering, management, pharmacy, law, and computer science degrees.
