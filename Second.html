About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering provides quality education in computing, programming, software development, and emerging technologies. Our goal is to prepare students for successful careers and lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in computer science education, research, innovation, and technological development for the benefit of society.

Our Mission

  1. To provide high-quality education in computer science and engineering.
  2. To develop technical skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities among students.
  3. To promote research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
  4. To encourage ethical practices and social responsibility.
